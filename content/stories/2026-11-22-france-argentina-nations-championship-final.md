France beat Argentina 45-17 at the Stade de France on Saturday to win the inaugural Nations Championship's northern hemisphere group and book a grand final against South Africa at Allianz Stadium in London on November 29.

Les Bleus knew before kickoff that only a bonus-point victory would be enough, England having beaten New Zealand 37-12 hours earlier. France led 21-10 at half-time, lock Mickael Guillard powering over for the opening score, and scored five tries in the second half for seven in all. Emilien Gailleton grounded the fourth try, the one that secured the bonus point, against 14 men after Mayco Vivas' yellow card was upgraded to a red. France finished top of the table on 26 points from five wins in six rounds; England and Ireland, also on five wins, took second and third on bonus points.

Antoine Dupont returned as captain at scrum-half, with Matthieu Jalibert reinstated at fly-half after a thigh injury. Argentina, who had lost their previous matches to sit bottom of the northern table, responded through a try from Justo Piccardo, and Tomas Albornoz's penalties put the visitors briefly ahead. Mayco Vivas went to the sin bin in the 61st minute and was later shown a red card; captain Augustin Montoya followed him to the bin in the 67th.

South Africa reached the final despite losing to Ireland 20-17 in Dublin. A losing bonus point drew the world champions level on 22 points with New Zealand, both on four wins, and the Springboks finished top of the southern hemisphere group on a points difference of plus 135 to plus 41. France beat South Africa 21-19 in Paris last week, the result that set up the rematch.

The finals weekend at Allianz Stadium begins Friday with Italy against Fiji for eleventh place and Ireland against Australia for fifth. England meet New Zealand again in the third-place final on Saturday, and Scotland play Argentina the following day. The championship final kicks off at 4.40 p.m. on Sunday.
