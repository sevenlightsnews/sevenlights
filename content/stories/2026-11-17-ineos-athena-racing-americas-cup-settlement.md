Sir Jim Ratcliffe's Ineos Group has reached an out-of-court settlement with Sir Ben Ainslie over the £180m yacht their companies built for the 2024 America's Cup, the companies said Monday.

Ineos Racing sued Athena Racing, a firm founded by Ainslie, in the High Court earlier this year, alleging a breach of the companies' agreement by refusal to return the yacht Ineos Britannia. Lawyers for Ineos valued the claim at more than £10 million in court documents. Athena defended the claim, arguing Ineos was not entitled to have the yacht back.

"Ineos has reached a settlement with Athena Racing and considers the matter now closed," Ineos said in a statement Monday. No further details, including whether the yacht has been returned, were provided.

Britannia carried Ainslie's campaign at the 2024 America's Cup in Barcelona, where Emirates Team New Zealand beat the Ineos challenge 7-2 in the final. The yacht had made Ainslie's crew the first British team to win the challenger series. Ratcliffe and Ainslie had worked closely across two Cup cycles before the partnership collapsed. Ineos said last year it had "reluctantly withdrawn its challenge" for the 2027 event after delays in reaching an agreement with Athena "undermined its ability to prepare" for the defence. In April 2025 Ainslie hit out at the Ineos Britannia organisation after it confirmed its Naples challenge would not involve him.

The filings showed how far the two sides were apart on ownership. Ineos said it had spent £180m on Britannia on top of £170m on the boat used in the 2021 campaign. Athena argued in its defence that it had always owned the assets and intellectual property from the two campaigns and that Ineos had been a sponsor. The suit was filed in March, after Ineos said it was "surprised" by Athena's decision to keep the yacht.

Ainslie's GB1 team now stands as challenger of record for the Naples regatta, which opens next year. No British team has won the America's Cup since the trophy was first contested in 1850. The settlement removes the last legal obstacle between the two camps as both turn to the 2027 campaign, Athena on the water and Ineos, the chemical group, elsewhere.
