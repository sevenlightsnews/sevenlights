Jermaine Haley, the Australian wrestler who performed for WWE as Bronson Reed, died November 26 of medical complications, the company announced Friday. He was 38. WWE said Haley was injured during its Crown Jewel event in Saudi Arabia this month and suffered multiple rib fractures and a lacerated kidney, according to multiple reports. He returned to the United States on November 18 for treatment.

In a statement, WWE called Haley "a larger-than-life figure" and "a devoted family man," extending condolences to his wife, the former wrestler Paige, who is pregnant, and their daughter. No cause of death was released beyond the company's reference to medical complications, and a statement from his family was not immediately available.

Haley wrestled on the Australian independent circuit before joining WWE in 2019. He built a main-roster act as "The Colossal," a heavy athlete known for a running splash from the top rope, and appeared at the company's major events including WrestleMania. His final match came against Oba Femi on an episode of NXT this month.

The death recalls the toll of WWE's expanding calendar of events in the Middle East. Crown Jewel drew criticism this month over the conditions of its card, and performers have raised concerns in recent years about travel strain, medical support on tour and the frequency of overseas stadium shows. Haley's injury and its aftermath renewed those questions on Friday, with former performers and reporters calling for disclosure of what occurred ringside in Saudi Arabia. WWE has not detailed the treatment Haley received at the event.

Regulatory oversight of professional wrestling varies by jurisdiction; athletic commissions license bouts in many American states, while overseas stadium events are generally outside their reach. Whether Saudi authorities open any inquiry into the incident was not known Friday.

Haley is expected to be remembered on WWE programming in the coming weeks, with tributes planned at the company's next television tapings. The company did not say whether Crown Jewel would return to Saudi Arabia next year.
