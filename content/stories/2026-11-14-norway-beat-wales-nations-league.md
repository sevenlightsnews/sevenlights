Norway beat Wales 3-1 at home Saturday in the UEFA Nations League, with Antonio Nusa, Andreas Schjelderup and Erling Haaland scoring after a goalless first half. The result leaves Wales guaranteed to finish last in Group A4.

Nusa opened the scoring in the 59th minute, collecting his own corner after a gymnastic first touch and finishing from the resulting scramble. Schjelderup doubled the lead in the 68th minute, sweeping home from a slick build-up in which Nusa again supplied the first pass. David Brooks pulled one back for Wales in the 79th minute, but a penalty awarded late against Ben Davies, who surrendered possession to Haaland, gave Haaland the spot-kick he converted in the 88th minute. Haaland has now scored 66 goals in 60 matches for Norway.

Wales were the better side through the first half, with Daniel James three times in chances that fell short of clear, but Norway's attacking quality told after the interval. Nusa troubled the Welsh defence all evening, beating Connor Roberts and Ethan Ampadu with a run in the closing minutes that produced further chances.

Portugal lead the group with 12 points from four matches. Norway and Denmark hold six points from five and four respectively, and Wales sit last on three. Having lost the head-to-head meetings with both Norway and Denmark, Wales cannot rise above either regardless of next week's result. Relegation is not yet confirmed: a fourth-placed side that ranks among the two best fourths across the league enters a two-legged play-off against a League B runner-up. Wales currently head that ranking on goal difference ahead of Serbia, Turkey and Czechia.

Wales travel to Portugal in Cardiff next week for their final match. "It's one of them … a sloppy first goal," defender Ben Davies told the BBC. "We could have been a bit more clinical … we have nothing to lose [against Portugal]." Norway finish the same matchday away, and hold second place in the group on goal difference ahead of Denmark.

Update, Nov. 15: Denmark beat Portugal 4-1 in Lisbon in the group's other Saturday match. Pierre-Emile Højbjerg scored from the penalty spot in the 43rd minute, Rasmus Højlund added two goals inside the opening hour and Joachim Andersen headed a fourth before Bernardo Silva's late consolation, the Guardian reported. Portugal, in their first match since Cristiano Ronaldo's retirement from international football, stay top of Group A4 on 12 points from five matches, three clear of Denmark.
