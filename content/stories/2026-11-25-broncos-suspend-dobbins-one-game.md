Denver Broncos head coach Sean Payton suspended running back J.K. Dobbins for Friday's game at the Pittsburgh Steelers for conduct detrimental to the team, announcing the decision Tuesday. Payton declined to describe the conduct, calling the suspension a "coach's decision."

"I think it's better that way," Payton said.

The suspension is for one game. Dobbins, who did not take part in practice Tuesday, will miss his first game of the season and fall short of a preseason pledge to play in all 17.

Dobbins, re-signed by Denver in the offseason on a two-year contract carrying $8m in guarantees, has been displaced as the lead running back by rookie Jonah Coleman. He played one snap in Sunday's win over the Las Vegas Raiders, has not played more than 14 snaps in any game since Week 6, and has 54 yards on 18 carries across the past four games.

Only a day earlier, Payton said Dobbins was healthy and would be "super important for what we're doing in this stretch."

Speaking earlier this month after Coleman took over the lead role in a win over Arizona, Dobbins said he accepted a reduced workload so long as the team won. "I like to consider myself a pro, right?" he said. "Now, if we're losing, and I'm not getting the carries, s—, I'm going to be mad as f—. Sorry, excuse my language. But if we're winning and it's working, I just want to win. I've been to the playoffs plenty of times. I just want to win a Super Bowl."

The Broncos enter Week 12 at 8-2 and face Pittsburgh on Black Friday afternoon. The Steelers, for that same game, benched their own veteran, quarterback Aaron Rodgers.

Denver has not announced whether the suspension carries a salary penalty or whether Dobbins will return to the lineup next week.
