![An industrial ethanol plant along a river at dusk, white storage tanks and pipe racks, police cars with amber lights on a wet access road, crime tape between bare trees, cold midwestern winter light, documentary realism, 3:2](/images/stories/peoria-ethanol-plant-bomb-defused.webp)

A one-mile evacuation zone around the BioUrja Renewables ethanol plant in Peoria, Illinois, was lifted at 1 p.m. local time Saturday after police defused a bomb found at the facility, city officials said. Residents in East Peoria were allowed to return home and businesses to resume normal operations.

Peoria police said a suspicious package was found near the plant at about 2 p.m. Friday. Officials determined hours later that it posed a credible threat. Police Chief Brad Dixon said the device was rendered safe at 9:12 p.m. Friday, and that searches for other possible devices continued across the wider area. "The area of BioUrja has not been rendered safe, just the package that was on site," Dixon said at a news conference Saturday. "It's a large facility; there's going to be an exhaustive search."

Dixon said there was still no explanation of what the device was. He said law enforcement agencies would continue the investigation and issue updates.

The FBI deployed personnel to work with the Peoria Police Department, the Peoria Fire Department, the Peoria County Sheriff's Department, the Illinois Secretary of State's Office and the Illinois State Police. "While it is early in the investigation, know this: we will work tirelessly to identify the individual responsible and bring them to justice," Special Agent Ryan Presley told reporters.

Governor JB Pritzker said on social media that he was monitoring the threat and had directed the Illinois State Police to coordinate with local authorities. Peoria Mayor Rita Ali told a local station that the facility stores ethanol and highly combustible chemicals, "and so that's what alarms us." The plant sits along the Illinois River in the southern part of the city, about 150 miles southwest of Chicago.

A country music concert scheduled nearby for Friday evening was cancelled. Police directed residents needing shelter to a nearby school.

No arrests were announced, and no motive had been established as of Saturday night. Federal and local agencies said the search of the plant and the identification of the device remained the immediate tasks.
