Ariana Grande said the reaction to her "Petal" music video resembled an episode of the television series Black Mirror, and that she would distribute the video again exactly as filmed.

The video, a satire following an aspiring actress named Pepper who is dissected by a panel of abusive men over her appearance, drew criticism that focused on Grande's own image in the clip — an inversion the singer called ironic.

"It's Black Mirror," Grande said on the Las Culturistas podcast in an episode released Wednesday. She called the reaction "interesting" and said: "I wouldn't change a thing. I would put it out again today, exactly as is."

Grande said she had not altered her work to suit expectation. "I've never changed my musical style or whatever, based on what I think people want from me. I've always moved authentically in that way. That's something I'm proud of." She added: "Let the chips fall where they may. My people will be my people."

"Petal" is the title track of Grande's eighth album, released in July. Describing the shoot, she said the set had been a deliberate gathering of women. "I remember how it felt in the room to be with all of those women dancers, and all of us discussing what it felt like and what it held for us," she said. "We've all been told some kind of interesting thing about ourselves that has nothing to do with the art or our talent or our work or what we've given to our audition — that has nothing to do with anything. So it was a very beautiful day on set, and it felt really good."

The interview came a day after Grande announced a two-part televised event chronicling her return to touring. The documentary "The Eternal Sunshine Diaries: The Making of the Tour" will follow the 30 days before the opening of the Eternal Sunshine tour, her first since 2019, and shows her finishing the Petal album on the road. It debuts on Peacock on December 11, followed on December 12 by the concert film "The Eternal Sunshine Tour."

The album and its campaign have drawn both strong sales and sustained argument about the video's imagery. Grande made no reference to the commercial figures.

The specials complete the Petal cycle. Nothing further has been announced for 2027.
