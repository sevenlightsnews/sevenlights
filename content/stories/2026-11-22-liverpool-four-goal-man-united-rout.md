Liverpool beat Manchester United 4-0 at Anfield on Sunday in Mauricio Pochettino's first match in charge of his old rivals. Leny Yoro was shown a straight red card in the eighth minute for bringing down Alexander Isak just outside the penalty area as the Sweden international ran clear, and United were a man down for all but the opening seven minutes thereafter.

Dominik Szoboszlai opened the scoring, and the lead was doubled three minutes later when Rio Ngumoha carried the ball past Diogo Dalot to the byline and Florian Wirtz finished from the resulting move. Victor Munoz and Wirtz added further goals as Liverpool dominated without strain. The referee was Sam Barrott.

The United defence unraveled quickly. Matthijs de Ligt, recently brought in, was at fault in three of the goals, losing sight of Isak on the move that produced the sending-off. Ngumoha troubled the United right side throughout, and the hosts pressed high from the first whistle on instructions to make the visitors feel pressure early. Pochettino responded by inserting Harry Maguire into a five-man defence, but United managed little in reply beyond sporadic set pieces.

The result leaves United in the Premier League relegation zone, the lowest position the club has occupied under a new manager's first month in decades of context that makes the fixture, historically the country's richest rivalry, a reckoning rather than a novelty. Liverpool recorded their fourth win by four goals or more against United since the start of the 2021-22 season.

Pochettino said his side must convert the "anger" of the defeat into "energy" for the work ahead, and acknowledged the scale of the rebuild at Old Trafford. Roy Keane, speaking on television, questioned his former club's "lack of fight."

United host their next fixture knowing the defensive selection question is unresolved, with Yoro suspended and Maguire's return to the starting role uncertain. Liverpool move on with a goal difference restored and the gap to the leaders narrowed.
