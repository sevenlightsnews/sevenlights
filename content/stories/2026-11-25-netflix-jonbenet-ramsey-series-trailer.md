Netflix released a trailer on Tuesday for "The Murder of JonBenét Ramsey," a ten-episode limited series starring Melissa McCarthy and Clive Owen as the parents of the six-year-old child beauty pageant contestant found dead in her Boulder, Colorado, home in 1996. All ten episodes release on 10 December.

The series, created by Harrison Query and Tommy Wallach with screenwriter Richard LaGravanese, reconstructs the media frenzy that followed the killing, in which Patsy and John Ramsey moved from grieving parents to objects of public suspicion as irregular details of the case surfaced. The case has never been solved.

In the trailer, an offscreen voice frames the enduring hold of the crime: "The whole country is looking for an end to this story that makes sense to them." As police close in on the family, Owen's John Ramsey denies involvement with flat composure, while McCarthy's Patsy Ramsey answers a proposed lie detector test by snapping, "I'll take ten of 'em."

Both actors said in promotion for the series that they approached it as a family tragedy rather than a puzzle. "It felt to me that if there was one thing that you could really hold onto as an anchor for the whole thing, it was the fact that it's a tragedy. It's not a whodunit, a little girl died," Owen said. McCarthy said: "We all forgot that these were parents and they loved their children. And one of them was taken in such an unimaginably violent, terrible way. That should be the crux of the story."

The cast also includes Margo Martindale, Clifton Collins Jr., Alison Pill, Garrett Hedlund, Shea Whigham, Owen Teague, Rory Cochrane, Chris Bauer, Angus Caldwell, Will Patton, John Billingsley, Jeremy Bobb, Jaime Ray Newman, Josh Stamberg, Tzi Ma, Kate Butler, Emily Mitchell and Grace Cashman.

The dramatization arrives three decades after the killing, which remains among the most enduring unsolved crimes in American history and has generated prior documentaries, television films and books. Netflix will release the full series on 10 December.
