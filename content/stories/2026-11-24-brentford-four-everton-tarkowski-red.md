Keane Lewis-Potter scored twice within three first-half minutes and Brentford beat Everton 4-0 at the Gtech Community Stadium on Sunday, the closing match of Premier League matchweek 11. Brentford moved to sixth, inside the European places, after 11 matches. Everton remain ninth.

Mr. Lewis-Potter's goals in the 23rd and 25th minutes came from a long ball that caught Everton out and from the chance that followed. Everton's captain, James Tarkowski, was sent off late in the first half for a sliding tackle on Yehor Yarmoliuk made with his studs raised. It was the first red card of his 351st Premier League appearance and his first sending-off since playing for Brentford in the Championship. Brentford were already 2-0 ahead, and the dismissal settled the contest.

Vitaly Janelt scored Brentford's third with a long-range strike in the 67th minute. Fabio Carvalho closed the scoring from close range in added time.

David Moyes, on the touchline for his 593rd game in charge of Everton, passed Harry Catterick alone in the club's record book. The milestone was overtaken by his side's defending. Everton sat in a low block that was not compact, lost the ball carelessly and failed to deal with Brentford's play down the wings, and offered little in attack.

Brentford led the line with Callum Wilson, who served as the lone forward with Igor Thiago suspended after a red card against Brighton. Everton's Jack Grealish made his first appearance in more than a month as a substitute.

Keith Andrews, Brentford's head coach, said his side were "outstanding" at 11 against 11 and set the tone they had asked for. "We want to be ambitious, we need everybody in the dressing room and the boys that came in tonight did really well," he said. Brentford travel to Manchester United on Saturday. Everton open a two-week stretch with the Merseyside derby at Goodison Park on Sunday.
