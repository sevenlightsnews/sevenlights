![Destroyed single-story school building with collapsed roof and rubble in a dusty courtyard, rescue workers in the distance, mountain foothills behind, hazy morning light, editorial photojournalism photograph, no faces, no text, 3:2](/images/stories/afghanistan-school-explosion-kunar.webp)

An explosion tore through a primary and middle school on the outskirts of Asadabad, the capital of eastern Afghanistan's Kunar province, on Saturday morning, killing 10 people and wounding 110, most of them children, officials said.

Interior ministry spokesman Abdul Mateen Qani put the death toll at 10 and said 110 had been wounded. Earlier tallies from provincial officials had put the dead at seven and the injured at as many as 99. Kunar police spokesman Farid Dehqan said the blast occurred at 10 a.m. local time in the Andar Ser area on the edge of Asadabad.

Kunar's director of culture and information, Sami ul Haq Bayan, said initial information indicated the explosion happened because of negligence at a depot next to the school that stored explosives belonging to mining companies.

The school was largely destroyed. Haji Sulaiman, whose son was wounded, said he was walking to his shop when he heard and saw the blast. "Pieces of wood and human flesh were flying everywhere," he said from his son's hospital bedside, adding that he ran toward the school. "When I arrived, I found children, some were dead, others were drenched in blood, and some rooftops had collapsed, the walls were gone, and nothing remained of the school."

Footage posted on social media showed cars carrying wounded children to the emergency department of a hospital in Asadabad. Footage from a hospital ward showed young children with bandaged heads, some lying two to a bed, while relatives sat at their sides. Photographers posted images of rescuers climbing through the collapsed shell of the building.

UNICEF said in a statement that it was "devastated" by the explosion, which it said had "claimed the lives of numerous children and left many others injured." Kunar is a mountainous province on Afghanistan's border with Pakistan. Bayan said an investigation into the blast was underway.
