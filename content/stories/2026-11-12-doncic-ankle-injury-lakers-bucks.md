Luka Doncic played through an injured ankle Wednesday and scored 32 points as the Lakers beat the Milwaukee Bucks 123-112 to open their road trip at 3-0, then said afterwards that the ankle hurt but that he would be fine.

Coach JJ Redick kept Doncic in the game with a 20-point lead and under five minutes remaining, and Doncic was hurt late. Los Angeles was outscored by 15 points in the stretch after he left. He finished with five rebounds, five assists and two steals in what he called a weak game by his standards.

"I'm good," Doncic said. "Obviously, it's painful a little bit, but I'll be fine. We got 3-0 on the road trip, so that's very good."

The Lakers, 11-2 and holding the NBA's best record, have seen Doncic battle minor injuries early in the season. He has also missed periodic stretches between December and February in recent years, a pattern the club had hoped to avoid this winter. Doncic entered Wednesday averaging 38.6 points, 7.6 assists and 7.5 rebounds.

The injury picture grew more complicated with the news that guard Austin Reaves also was hurt against the Bucks. The Athletic reported Reaves suffered a heel problem and would be evaluated. With two ball-handlers banged up at once, the Lakers face a rotation problem on a trip that continues this week: Redick may lack the depth to rest both guards at the same time.

Imaging on the ankle was not immediately required, and Redick said the staff would decide on Doncic and Reaves day by day. No timeline was announced for either player.

Doncic's availability for the trip's next game had not been determined as of Wednesday night. The Lakers hold the league's best record by a game, and the remaining road dates will test the health of the backcourt before the schedule turns back homeward.
