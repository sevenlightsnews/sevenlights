Morgan Rogers and Harry Kane scored as England beat Croatia 2-1 at Wembley on Thursday, securing a place in the quarter-finals of the UEFA Nations League.

Rogers opened the scoring in the 24th minute. Nikola Sučić equalized for Croatia in the 59th. Kane restored the lead in the 64th from the penalty spot, his 92nd international goal in a record 126th appearance.

It was England's third win over Croatia this year and, by the Guardian's account, their least authoritative display of the three. The manager, Thomas Tuchel, told ITV that the team "took us a while to grow into the game" but settled after a quarter of an hour. "We deserved it ... we created enough ... we got the job done," he said. "It was important because it was a special occasion for Harry and he scored the winner so a perfect evening."

Rogers said of his captain: "He scores a goal as he always does ... he's relentless ... he doesn't get there by accident." Kane called the pre-match reception, which included a large tifo display, "an incredible gesture," and noted that he had missed his last penalty for Bayern Munich. "It's always important to bounce back with the next one," he said.

Spain beat the Czech Republic 2-0 on Thursday, a first-half penalty from Álex Baena and a goal deep in injury time by Marc Pubill, and lead Group A3 three points above England. Spain need only a draw in Sunday's meeting to finish first. A win would take England level on points and on head-to-head with the world champions, and top the group on goal difference.

Croatia sit third in Group A3 and, under the competition format, face a play-off in March to avoid relegation. The knockout rounds begin that month with two-legged quarter-finals; the winners advance to one-off semi-finals played on June 9 and 10, 2027.
