The Detroit Lions beat the New England Patriots 35-10 in Munich on Sunday for their fifth consecutive victory. Both teams lost key players to injuries: Patriots quarterback Drake Maye hurt an ankle on the opening drive, and Lions pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson injured a knee while celebrating the sack that ended Maye's afternoon.

Amon-Ra St Brown caught two touchdown passes in his German homecoming. A 56-yard strike in the first quarter gave Detroit a 7-3 lead, and a 28-yard reception in the third quarter, after teammates pushed him over the goal line in a scrum, made it 28-3. St Brown, whose mother is German and who spent childhood summers in the country, arrived at the stadium in lederhosen and carried a German flag onto the field.

Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 153 yards and two touchdowns and added a 50-yard reception. He scored from five yards out one play after the catch, and finished the scoring with a 28-yard run in the fourth quarter. Gibbs became the first NFL player since Priest Holmes in 2002 with more than 150 rushing yards, two rushing touchdowns and at least 95 receiving yards in a game. Jared Goff completed 20 of 31 passes for 303 yards with three touchdowns and two interceptions, finding Isaac TeSlaa for a 12-yard score capping a 97-yard drive.

Maye was hurt late on New England's first drive and did not return; further tests are scheduled. Hutchinson, who injured his right knee in the celebration of the sack, is believed to have suffered a serious injury, according to NFL Network reporters. Lions coach Dan Campbell said the staff would know more on return to the United States. "Anytime you lose a player like that and you don't know the entirety of it ... it hurts a little bit," Campbell said.

The Lions improved to 7-2 and sit atop their division. The Patriots fell to 5-5, their second straight loss and their third in four games, with their season now pivoting on the health of their rookie quarterback.

Both teams return to the United States for further examinations this week.
