Fifa awarded the 2035 Women's World Cup to a joint bid from the football associations of England, Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales on Monday, confirming Britain's first global football tournament since the men's World Cup of 1966. The bid was approved by acclamation at an Extraordinary Fifa Congress held virtually.

The 2031 tournament was awarded on the same day to an uncontested bid from the United States, Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica. Both tournaments are set at 48 teams and follow the format used at the 2026 men's World Cup. The 2035 competition is scheduled to run from June 21 to July 29 and is expected to feature 104 matches.

The UK bid lists 22 stadiums as potential venues: 16 in England, three in Wales, two in Scotland and one in Northern Ireland. Fifa's evaluation report rated 19 of the grounds as high quality, scoring all but Elland Road at least four out of five. Wembley, which staged the 2022 Women's European Championship final won by England, is proposed for the final. Windsor Park in Belfast would be expanded to 23,752 seats from 18,000. Three further grounds could be added: Birmingham's planned 60,000-seat Powerhouse Stadium, Wrexham's redeveloped Racecourse Ground and a stadium in London.

The bid team predicted more than 4.5 million ticket sales, more than double the women's World Cup record set in 2023, and a projected television audience of 3.5 billion viewers. It claimed 63 million people live within two hours of at least one proposed venue.

The UK bid's presentation was led not by the four chief executives but by a Sky Sports News presenter and four schoolchildren, one from each country. FA sources rejected suggestions this distanced the associations from Fifa's president, Gianni Infantino, who presided over the congress; all four associations called for his resignation in the summer. After the award, the FA chair, Debbie Hewitt, embraced Mr. Infantino and promised an "exceptional" World Cup.

"Andy Burnham, the Greater Manchester mayor, said the tournament would "bring more people into the game and leave a legacy that lasts long after the final whistle."

Host cities will be confirmed in a later process. The bid's stadium list is not final.
