A federal grand jury in Los Angeles indicted Thomas Markle, the father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, on Tuesday on two counts of possessing child sexual abuse material, the U.S. attorney's office said. Each count carries a maximum of 20 years in prison if convicted.

Court documents filed in California say the materials include at least one image involving a prepubescent minor and a minor under 12 years old. The indictment, brought by the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Central District of California, accuses Markle of knowingly possessing 35mm film negatives on 30 October and 4 November that contained such material.

Federal agents found the material during a search of a storage unit Markle had allegedly rented in Los Angeles for almost two decades. Inside, they recovered two images depicting an adult man — identified by a witness as Markle — with an underage child, and a third image that appears to depict a child in a room believed to be part of a motel chain in the Philippines. Prosecutors said the affidavit also describes additional photographs that appear to show Markle with children in various stages of undress.

Markle, 82, was arrested by the FBI on 6 November at an assisted-living facility in Florida, where he has been a patient. He was initially released on conditional bail and was ordered to remain in jail after allegedly removing a location-monitoring device days after his arrest. Last week a U.S. magistrate judge in Tampa ruled, following a court-ordered psychiatric evaluation, that he was mentally competent to stand trial. He is expected to be transferred to California in the coming weeks for arraignment in U.S. District Court.

U.S. Attorney Bill Essayli announced the charges. Markle has denied possessing the images. Philippine police said last week that criminal complaints had been filed against him in Cebu province, where he lived until May after a series of strokes, accusing him of violating local laws against online sexual abuse and exploitation of children. He told the Daily Mail the Philippine claims were untrue and described them as "sick lies."

The duchess, an American former actress, has been estranged from her father since the run-up to her 2018 marriage to Prince Harry. She has said she became aware of allegations against him at that time and was "deeply distressed," a matter that led her to end contact between her father and her two children, Archie, 7, and Lilibet, 5.

Markle remains in custody in Florida. His arraignment in Los Angeles is expected after his transfer.
