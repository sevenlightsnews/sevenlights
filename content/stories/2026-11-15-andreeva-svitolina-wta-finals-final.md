Mirra Andreeva beat Jessica Pegula 6-3, 6-4 on Saturday to reach the final of the WTA Finals at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden, and will play Ukraine's Elina Svitolina for the title on Sunday.

Andreeva, 19, has not dropped a set in the round-robin stage or the semifinals. She has conceded 18 games in four matches, including a 6-4, 6-0 win over the top seed, Aryna Sabalenka, in which she took 11 consecutive games from 1-4 down. The world No. 3 will rise to a career-best No. 2 in the rankings on Monday regardless of the final's outcome. A year ago she failed to qualify for the year-end field, missing it by six points.

Svitolina reached the final with a 6-2, 6-0 win over the Wimbledon champion, Linda Noskova, in the second semifinal. She also beat Sabalenka this week. She is seeking the WTA Finals title for the second time after winning in 2018. She and Andreeva met in the group stage, which Andreeva won 6-4, 6-0.

Sabalenka apologised on social media on Saturday after being accused of making offensive remarks toward Svitolina during Friday's round-robin match. Camera footage caught her speaking between points, and some on social media interpreted the comment as an insult directed at her Ukrainian opponent.

"My comment was not directed at Elina," Sabalenka wrote. "I was frustrated with a small group of spectators who were aggressively cheering my mistakes, and I reacted emotionally in the moment. That said, I should have handled my frustration differently, and I apologise to anyone I offended. Elina played a great match, and I wish her all the best for the rest of the tournament."

The final carries consequences beyond the trophy. Elena Rybakina, the world No. 1, and Sabalenka defend large numbers of points at the Australian Open after meeting in Melbourne's final this year. Andreeva, with fewer points to defend, opens the 2027 season within reach of the top ranking if she adds the biggest title of her career.
