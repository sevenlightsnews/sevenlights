Thomas Markle Sr., the 82-year-old father of Meghan, the Duchess of Sussex, was jailed without bond after he cut an electronic monitoring device from his ankle days after his arrest on federal charges of possessing child sexual abuse material, the Justice Department said Tuesday.

Mr. Markle, the estranged father of the duchess, was arrested in Florida last week after authorities raided California storage units allegedly registered in his name and found the material. A magistrate had placed him under house detention, ordered him to wear a location-monitoring device and set the terms of his release pending trial. Prosecutors said he removed the monitor early Tuesday morning.

He appeared before US Magistrate Judge Lindsay S. Griffin in Tampa on Tuesday and was denied bond. Judge Griffin found that Mr. Markle had violated the conditions of his pretrial release and described his housing situation as "untenable." "Based on the lack of suitable housing, no condition or combination of conditions would reasonably assure the safety of any other person or the community and the defendant's appearance in court," she wrote in the order revoking his release. The judge also ordered a medical evaluation and directed that Mr. Markle "receive all necessary and prescribed medication unless discontinued by a physician."

Mr. Markle has denied possessing the images. "Release conditions are court orders, not suggestions," Bill Essayli, the US attorney for the Central District of California, wrote on X on Tuesday.

CBS News reported that Mr. Markle is back in federal custody following the alleged removal of the device. The Los Angeles Times reported that the monitor was cut away from his ankle while he awaited a hearing on the charges.

Cutting or disabling a court-ordered device is itself a violation of release conditions and commonly results in revocation of release, as it did here. A hearing on the charges and the release violation is pending, and no date was announced.
