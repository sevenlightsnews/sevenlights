Crystal Palace beat Real Sociedad 2-1 in the Europa League at Selhurst Park on Thursday, coming from a goal down in the final half-hour for Thomas Frank's first victory as the club's manager.

The Premier League's bottom club, defeated by Coventry City in Mr. Frank's opening game Saturday, controlled little of the first half and offered less in attack. The visitors broke the deadlock in the 66th minute when Chelsea loanee Mamadou Sarr scored. Six minutes later the match tipped: Jon Martin was shown a second yellow card and Real Sociedad finished with 10 men.

Jean-Philippe Mateta levelled from the penalty spot, and Dwight McNeil poked Palace ahead in the 82nd minute from substitute Jorgen Strand Larsen's bicycle kick. Yeremy Pino then had a second penalty saved by Unai Marrero, and Orri Oskarsson nearly forced a late equaliser before Dean Henderson pushed his effort away, his last of six saves.

"I think first and foremost to get it over the line, and (it was) an extremely important win for various reasons," Mr. Frank said. "One, of course, confidence, and it relates to belief against an extremely good team." He said the first half had been compact and disciplined but poor in keeping the ball, and that the second had been better until "out of the blue, they scored the 1-0 goal." He said his team had come close to giving him a "heart attack" in the closing minutes.

The tactical shift from his predecessor, Pierre Sage, was visible. Where Sage considered retaining possession the best defence, Mr. Frank's team concentrated on defending at all costs until the sending-off opened the game. Defensive set pieces had been drilled in detail in training, and Mr. Frank said the club must not underestimate its threat from dead balls.

The result lifted Palace into a play-off position in the Europa League, four places out of the top eight after five matches. Since a Conference League defeat by AEK Larnaca in October 2025, they have been unbeaten in nine European home games, winning six.

The league remains the priority, and the fixture list offers no rest. Palace host Hull City on Sunday without Pino and Mateta, suspended on yellow-card accumulation, and with Ismaila Sarr out weeks with a groin injury. They have won three league home games in more than a year.
