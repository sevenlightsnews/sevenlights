Arion Kurtaj, the 21-year-old who leaked gameplay footage of Grand Theft Auto VI in 2022, has pleaded guilty to charges arising from the intrusion, the Crown Prosecution Service said.

The prosecution account traces his activity to the months between August and November 2021, when Kurtaj hacked the telecommunications company BT/EE. The company noted "a marked increase in unauthorised SIM swaps" of customers' mobile phones that November, in which numbers are transferred to a device in the attacker's possession to intercept account verifications.

Charged over those offences, Kurtaj was moved to a hotel in September 2022 for his own protection. From there he breached his bail conditions to carry out further intrusions on major companies. The Crown Prosecution Service said he worked with a mobile phone, the hotel television's Wi-Fi connection and a streaming media stick. The targets included Rockstar Games, the ride-hailing company Uber and the payments firm Revolut.

At Rockstar, Kurtaj obtained and published 90 videos of in-development footage from Grand Theft Auto VI, long before the game's scheduled release on November 19. He first tried to extort the developer, then released the footage and some source code. The leak humiliated the studio and prompted stricter security, including a ban on developers working from home. Another leaker released further gameplay videos this summer, which Rockstar described as heartbreaking.

Doctors found Kurtaj unfit to stand trial in 2023 because of acute autism, and after a jury determined he had committed the earlier crimes, a judge sentenced him to indefinite hospitalisation in December 2023. He was deemed fit for trial in January and moved to a standard prison. At Woolwich Crown Court in London on Monday he pleaded guilty to the Rockstar hack and admitted further offences committed as a teenager with the Lapsus$ hacking group.

Sentencing is set for November 23, four days after the game's release.
