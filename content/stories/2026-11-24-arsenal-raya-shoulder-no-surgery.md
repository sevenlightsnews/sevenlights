David Raya will not require surgery on the shoulder he dislocated at Newcastle on Saturday, and Arsenal's manager, Mikel Arteta, said Monday that the injury is "not too serious." The goalkeeper will miss Tuesday's Champions League match against Borussia Dortmund at the Emirates Stadium.

Mr. Raya was forced off in the second half of the 2-2 draw at St James' Park with a suspected dislocation, an injury that raised fears of an extended absence. He underwent an MRI scan after the squad returned to London and was absent from training Monday. Kepa Arrizabalaga is expected to start in his place against Dortmund.

"We were worried," Mr. Arteta said. "He's better than expected." He confirmed that no operation is needed and said Mr. Raya could return for the match against Manchester City. A timetable was not given.

Mr. Raya was helped from the field by Simon Murphy, Arsenal's head of physiotherapy and rehabilitation, in the second half at St James' Park on Nov. 21. Mr. Arteta said at a news conference that further tests would establish the exact extent of the damage. Asked whether the goalkeeper could play against Manchester City, he said: "Tomorrow we'll have more tests and we will know."

The fixture list leaves little room for a prolonged absence. Arsenal host Borussia Dortmund, Brentford and Tottenham Hotspur and travel to Manchester City and Real Madrid in their next five matches. Dortmund sit one point behind Arsenal in the Champions League league phase after winning three of their first four games, and their coach, Niko Kovac, leads Bayern Munich by two points at the top of the German league.

Mr. Raya's role in Arsenal's build-up play from the back has made him central to the way the team plays. Mr. Arteta said the club was "very protective" of Mr. Arrizabalaga and the third-choice keeper, Illan Meslier. "Everybody needs to be ready because these things happen," he said.
