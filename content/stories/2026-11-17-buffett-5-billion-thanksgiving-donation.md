Warren Buffett is donating about $5.47 billion in Berkshire Hathaway shares to four family foundations, Berkshire announced Monday. The chairman emeritus, 96, will convert 6,150 Class A shares into 9,225,000 Class B shares and distribute them among the organizations run by his three children.

The Susan Thompson Buffett Foundation receives 3,690,000 shares, or 40 per cent of the gift, worth roughly $2.19 billion. The Sherwood Foundation, the Howard G. Buffett Foundation and the NoVo Foundation receive 1,845,000 shares each, about $1.09 billion apiece at Monday's valuation. The dollar figures move with Berkshire's share price.

"Today's gift is consistent with the plan I have previously described," Buffett wrote. "All my Berkshire shares will be donated to the four foundations, with the goal of growing the grants each year." He cautioned that the goal depends largely on market conditions between now and his target date, which the message did not specify. After the donation he will retain 182,140 Class A shares and 1,162 Class B shares.

Buffett paired the gift with his annual Thanksgiving message, shorter than last year's. "Last Thanksgiving I wrote that kindness is costless but also priceless," he wrote. "A year later, I would only add that it compounds, and at a better rate than anything Charlie and I ever bought."

He named teachers including Ben Graham, Joe Rosenfield and Tom Murphy, and asked readers to thank a selfless person in their own lives, or "be a benefactor for someone else."

The gift steps up his annual giving and transfers stock rather than cash, leaving the foundations' assets tied to the company Buffett led for six decades. He said his confidence in Berkshire, which his son Howard succeeded him as chairman in September, remains "as strong as ever." Berkshire shares have risen more than 19 per cent since the start of the fourth quarter, closing at record highs four times last week.

The donation advances but does not complete the disposition of his stake. The remaining shares are destined for the same four foundations.
