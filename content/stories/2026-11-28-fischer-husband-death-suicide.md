The office of Senator Deb Fischer of Nebraska announced Friday that Bruce Fischer, the longtime husband of the Republican senator, died by suicide, revising an initial statement that had said he "passed away peacefully" nearly a week ago.

Senator Fischer first released a statement on X on Sunday. It announced that her husband of more than 50 years had died the day before the post, "doing what he loved the most, spending time outdoors." She wrote in that statement that she would "cherish the decades we spent building a family in the Nebraska Sandhills."

"Above all, Bruce was a loving husband, a devoted father and grandfather, a man of faith and a rancher," the senator wrote on Nov. 23.

On Friday morning a spokesperson released a further statement describing a "sad update in the death" of Bruce Fischer. "Initially, it was believed that Bruce passed peacefully at home," the statement on X read. "Upon further investigation, it was learned that Bruce chose to end his life."

The spokesperson did not disclose details about the suicide or how the determination was made.

Senator Fischer, a Republican who has served in the Senate since 2012, represents Nebraska and sits among the chamber's senior members from the Great Plains states. Her husband was a rancher, and the family's Sandhills homestead has been a fixture of her political biography throughout her tenure.

Bruce Fischer drew national attention in 2025 when he appeared to refuse a handshake from then Vice President Kamala Harris at a public event in Nebraska.

News organizations covering the death noted the senator's original account and the subsequent correction. Statements from colleagues in the Senate were requested; her office directed inquiries to the family's wishes for privacy.

The case drew attention to how public figures disclose causes of death. Reporting on suicide is governed in newsrooms by long-standing guidance to state the fact plainly, avoid describing method and note help resources, because detailed coverage is associated with increased risk of imitation.
