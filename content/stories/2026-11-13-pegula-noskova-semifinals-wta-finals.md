Jessica Pegula beat Linda Noskova 7-6 (7-2), 4-6, 7-5 on Thursday at the WTA Finals in Indian Wells, California, saving a match point in the deciding set of a contest that lasted two hours and 45 minutes.

Noskova had gone a break up in the third set and held match point on Pegula's serve at 5-4. Pegula held, broke back and closed out one of the matches of the year. She served a career-high 16 aces; Noskova, the Wimbledon champion, committed a career-high 15 double faults.

The win sent Pegula into the semifinals and eliminated Coco Gauff. Noskova had already secured her place in the last four before Pegula's win, and finished first in the group with a record of two matches won and one lost. She led the group on set count: five sets won to two lost, against five to three for Pegula and four to three for Gauff. The top two in each round-robin group advance to the semifinals. Gauff had beaten Marta Kostyuk 6-4, 7-5 earlier Thursday, winning six games in a row after dropping to 1-5 in the second set and receiving treatment on her back during it. She served nine double faults against one ace. It was the second time this week she recovered from a 1-5 set deficit; she did the same against Pegula on Sunday.

Kostyuk joined the eight-player field as an alternate after world number one Elena Rybakina withdrew because of illness. She failed to win a set in three matches while struggling with a wrist injury.

Pegula plays French Open champion Mirra Andreeva in Saturday's semifinals, their first meeting. Noskova faces the winner of Friday's round-robin match between Aryna Sabalenka and Elina Svitolina.

The tournament was moved from Riyadh to the California desert this year because of the US-Israeli war on Iran. Pegula, playing before a home crowd, next faces a teenager who has carried her own controversy into the week: a hot-microphone exchange involving Andreeva has shadowed the buildup to their match.
