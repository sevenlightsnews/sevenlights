The Writers Guild of America posted official credits for "Avengers: Doomsday" on Thursday, naming Stephen McFeely and Michael Waldron as the film's writers and crediting eight others with additional literary material, among them Ryan Reynolds and Hugh Jackman.

The guild's "and" credit between McFeely and Waldron means the two did not write as a team. The additional-literary-material list, which will not be seen on screen, also includes Eric Pearson, Chris McKenna, Erik Sommers, Jeff Loveness, Andrew Lanham and Destin Daniel Cretton. Jackman does not appear previously to have held guild membership, the trade press noted.

The listing of the two actors' names all but confirms their appearance in the film. Reynolds appeared at San Diego Comic-Con in July in a Deadpool outfit and asked Kevin Feige if he could be in the film; Jackman appeared by video at Disney's D23 presentation in August and tacitly confirmed his participation. Marvel has not formally confirmed the duo's involvement. Reynolds responded to the credit reveal on Thursday, further fueling the speculation.

The film introduces Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom and brings together the characters of "Thunderbolts*" and "The Fantastic Four: First Steps" while introducing new versions of the X-Men, whom the studio hopes will become its next major franchise. Several of the writers credited with additional material have recently written or directed the standalone films of those characters. Even if the actors' contributions amounted largely to improvisation, the Playlist judged them sufficient to warrant guild credit. The guild posted the full credit list on its website.

"Doomsday" opens Dec. 18. Speculation over the film's cameos has run high; Anthony Mackie recently spoiled the return of Paul Bettany's Vision, and talk that the finalized guild credits would tip the appearances of two popular characters has now been borne out.

Marvel has not commented on the credits.
