Macaulay Culkin is in talks to return as Kevin McCallister in a new Home Alone film for Disney and 20th Century Studios, multiple outlets reported Friday. Seth Rogen and his writing and producing partner Evan Goldberg have closed a deal on a script built from an idea Culkin pitched to the studio in recent months.

The story would center on Kevin as a father, with the boy left to defend the home again. Puck reported the sixth installment would reunite Culkin with his most famous role. Culkin was spotted at Walt Disney Studios in Burbank, California, in July to discuss the project.

The original film, written by John Hughes and directed by Chris Columbus, made Culkin a star at eight in 1990; he reprised the role in Columbus's 1992 sequel, Home Alone 2: Lost in New York. The two grossed a combined $836 million worldwide. Culkin and Columbus left the franchise after the second film. Hughes returned to write Home Alone 3 (1997), which starred Alex D. Linz; a made-for-television fourth film followed in 2002 with Mike Weinberg, and a fifth, Home Alone: The Holiday Heist, in 2012 with Christian Martyn. The most recent entry, Home Sweet Home Alone, went to Disney+ in 2021.

Columbus, 68, delivered the franchise's sharpest verdict while marking the original's 35th anniversary in December 2025. "Sorry to insult anybody, but they've completely f---ed it up," he told The Hollywood Reporter. "It started with Home Alone 3, and then it just went downhill from there." He said he liked the idea of an older Kevin, "like a widower or something like that." Columbus is at work on Gremlins 3, a legacy sequel to his other holiday hit.

Reps for Disney and Culkin did not immediately respond to requests for comment. No director or release date has been set.

The 1990 film held the live-action comedy box-office record for nearly two decades. No director or release date has been set for the sequel.

Update, November 22, 2026: The Playlist reported that the long-discussed sequel is now officially in development, with Rogen and Goldberg's Point Grey producing. The earlier reports had described the project as in talks.
