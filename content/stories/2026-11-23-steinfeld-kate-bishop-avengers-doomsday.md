Marvel Studios confirmed on Sunday that Hailee Steinfeld will return as Kate Bishop in "Avengers: Doomsday," the next ensemble film in its flagship franchise.

The announcement arrived without a casting reveal or a studio presentation. Marvel Studios and Walt Disney Animation posted a video on Instagram featuring Steinfeld, who is in Europe promoting the animated film "Hexed." The post begins with her noting she came to Europe for that picture. "Hey, so I'm here with my movie 'Hexed,'" she says, before acknowledging that the studios delivered news of another kind, Variety reported. The Playlist called it a quiet confirmation of a familiar face.

Deadline described the device as "a nifty bit of cross promotion": Disney used its surefire blockbuster and a helpful coincidence to reveal an Avengers casting while promoting a smaller animated title.

Steinfeld has played Kate Bishop, a young archer who takes the Hawkeye name from Clint Barton, since the 2021 Disney+ series "Hawkeye," which paired her with Jeremy Renner's Barton. In the comics, Bishop was created by the writer Matt Fraction and the artist David Aja and introduced in 2012 as the second character to hold the Hawkeye identity. She has not appeared in a theatrical release.

"Doomsday" follows "Infinity War" and "Endgame," released in 2018 and 2019, as the franchise's next Avengers film. Further casting for the ensemble has been disclosed in fragments over the past year, with confirmations arriving through interviews, industry events and social media rather than a single cast list.

The confirmation closes the question of Bishop's return that had followed the character since the series ended. A full roster for the film has yet to be published.

For "Hexed," the pairing supplied an audience of a different order: the Avengers announcement carried the animated film's promotion into the holiday corridor, where studios compete for franchise attention. No further details on "Doomsday" were released Sunday.
