The finale of "VisionQuest," the Marvel Television miniseries on Disney+, ended Wednesday with a post-credits scene. It reveals that Wanda Maximoff survived the collapse of the Scarlet Witch temple and has been found by Doctor Doom, binding the series directly to "Avengers: Doomsday," due in about a month.

The scene continues the climactic sequence of the 2022 film "Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness," in which a grief-stricken Maximoff brought the temple on Mount Wundagore down upon herself. In the tag, green magic lifts a piece of debris and a hooded figure speaks in a raspy voice: "Scarlet Witch, awaken. Your power will be mine." Maximoff opens her eyes as the screen cuts to black. The figure is Victor Von Doom, played by Robert Downey Jr., the antagonist of "Doomsday."

The finale itself opened with the reunion of the now-adult Tommy Maximoff, played by Ruaridh Mollica, and his long-lost twin brother Billy, played by Joe Locke, whose survival was explained in the spinoff "Agatha All Along." The return of the siblings and of their synthezoid father, Vision, played by Paul Bettany in his third incarnation, prompts Tommy to wonder aloud whether Maximoff might return as well. A second tag showed the human Ultron, played by James Spader, pondering what to do with the saved hard drive of Jocasta, played by T'Nia Miller.

The tag answers a standing question of the franchise's current era: whether Maximoff, played by Elizabeth Olsen, would appear in the next Avengers films. A similar scene attached to the rerelease "Avengers: Endgame Encore" showed Bruce Banner being "collected," and the "VisionQuest" tease supports persistent reports that the cloaked "Latverian Witches" glimpsed in "Doomsday" trailers include Maximoff.

"VisionQuest" capped the WandaVision trilogy that began in 2020. Episodes were directed by Christopher J. Byrne, Gandja Monteiro, Vincenzo Natali and Terry Matalas. The series carried two credits scenes, of which the resurrection tag was the consequential one.

"Doomsday" arrives next month, with "Secret Wars" scheduled after a year's intermission. Whether the events of "VisionQuest" carry into the films has not been confirmed by Marvel.
