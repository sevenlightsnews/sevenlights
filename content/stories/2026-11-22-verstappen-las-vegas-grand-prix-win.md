Max Verstappen won the Las Vegas Grand Prix on Saturday night by six-tenths of a second, holding off Lando Norris for fifty laps to take his third victory on the streets of Nevada. The Red Bull driver crossed the line in one hour, 27 minutes and 39.624 seconds, ahead of Norris and McLaren teammate Oscar Piastri.

Verstappen started from pole position and led from the first lap, but the new regulations produced a bunched field and a race of classic slipstreaming on the wide strip straights. George Russell swept past him on the Strip by lap two; Verstappen retook the lead at turn 17, the final corner, and the pair repeated the pattern lap after lap. Russell attacked again on lap 14 and passed, and again lost the place at turn 17. The top ten car was within five seconds of the leader for much of the night, and the lead changed hands repeatedly.

Kimi Antonelli joined the fight, passing Russell for second and then taking the lead from Verstappen before the Dutchman answered back. The closing laps decided the podium. Russell locked up after running over debris and fell to ninth, and Antonelli was losing power at the crucial moment when both McLarens passed him. He finished fourth, two and a half seconds behind. Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto took fifth and sixth for Alpine.

Ferrari's night unraveled again. Lewis Hamilton served a ten-second penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage in an earlier battle with Norris, made contact with Charles Leclerc later, and finished seventh. Leclerc received a ten-second penalty of his own for the collision. Team principal Fred Vasseur faces another urgent debrief after the pair were reprimanded for clashing in Mexico. A mid-race safety car followed a collision between Sergio Perez and Rafael Camara, and Isack Hadjar dropped back after damaging his front wing.

For Verstappen the win was built on defense rather than pace. Once he held the lead from the off, he was hunted to the flag, and no rival kept the place for more than a few corners. It was the fourth-time world champion's third win in Las Vegas and the Red Bull driver's strongest performance of a season in which the field has rarely been closer.
