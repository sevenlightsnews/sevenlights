Marvel released a final trailer for "Avengers: Doomsday" on Monday, two months before the film's Dec. 18 opening, and in it the studio's three franchises finally share a battlefield. The X-Men, the Fantastic Four and a roster of Avengers both new and old converge on the army of Sentinels serving Doctor Doom, played by Robert Downey Jr.

The trailer answers the question that has shadowed the marketing since casting was announced: why Steve Rogers, played by Chris Evans, is in the film. It shows him drawn out of a quiet domestic life with Peggy Carter, played by Hayley Atwell, and their son. Scott Summers, played by James Marsden, is shown fighting Sam Wilson, played by Anthony Mackie, before the two sides are ranged together.

The closing shot supplies the sequence's most promoted moment. Mr. Marsden's Cyclops delivers the line "To me, my X-Men," heard in live action for the first time in 26 years, cueing a circling group shot that recalls the 2012 ensemble film. Gambit, Nightcrawler, Mystique and Beast then advance against Doom's Sentinels alongside characters from outside the X-Men's ranks, including Yelena Belova, Shang-Chi, Thor and Reed Richards. Figures absent from the franchise for years, among them Kate Bishop, also appear. The trailer indicates that Scarlet Witch fights on Doom's side.

Earlier trailers for the film had introduced its cast separately and rarely staged the three teams together, which had left the film's central promise untested on screen. The new cut places that collision at the center of the campaign.

"Avengers: Doomsday" is the next chapter in the franchise's ongoing multiverse arc and the first film to bring the X-Men, acquired with Twentieth Century Fox in 2019, into the ensemble. The Russo brothers direct.

A further marketing push is expected in December. The film runs in theaters worldwide on Dec. 18.
