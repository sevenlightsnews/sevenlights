Nottingham Forest beat Chelsea 3-2 in the Premier League on Friday night, deepening Chelsea's worst run of the season and lifting Forest out of the relegation zone. Morgan Gibbs-White scored twice, and Igor Jesus opened the scoring with a volley that left the visiting goalkeeper grounded.

Jesus finished high into the net in the opening minutes, and Chelsea offered little answer before half-time. Wesley Fofana gave away a penalty with a wild challenge, and Gibbs-White converted on the 51st minute. Emanuel Emegha pulled one back for Chelsea three minutes later, the first of two goals he took from almost nothing in a match Chelsea otherwise dominated on paper and lost in the essentials. Gibbs-White's second, on the 73rd minute, settled the contest: a smooth chip over the keeper after Xaver Schlager's through ball. Emegha added a second late, and Forest held on.

The result leaves Chelsea eighth with 16 points from 12 matches, having lost six league games this season and three in a row. Chelsea were borderline incompetent in defence and impotent in attack other than the two openings that produced Emegha's goals.

Forest rose from 17th to 14th with 12 points under Oliver Glasner, appointed this autumn, and back-to-back home wins at the City Ground against Manchester City and Chelsea. Gibbs-White, the two-goal hero, said the conditions suited the occasion: "These are what the City nights are all about … the lights … raining … bad weather … the team showed up … grit … determination … fighting spirit … we got the job done, deservedly." On the pressure around his manager, he added: "We have a new manager, we're sticking with him, we trust the process." Glasner said he remained fully supported by the club's ownership. A downcast Xabi Alonso told Sky Sports: "It's a difficult moment for everyone … the club, the team, the fans … for sure it's disappointing to lose again … we need more wins and then we will start to build on this foundation."

Chelsea play a Champions League fixture midweek before returning to league duty, with the table now crowded around them. Forest carry the momentum of consecutive home points into next weekend's fixture.
