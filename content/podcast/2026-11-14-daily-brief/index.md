It is the first three-way meeting among the three leaders, and the White House confirmed it on Friday. The session will close Trump's visit to China. It takes place on the margins of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit, hosted in the technology hub. White House spokesperson Olivia Wales said Trump would discuss reducing nuclear weapons. Trump rejected Moscow's call for a one-year extension. He wants a new and modernized deal that includes China. Pentagon estimates put China's arsenal above six hundred warheads. 3|Closer to home, the Federal Aviation Administration halted every departure in the United States on Friday afternoon. Aircraft sat on the ground for roughly four hours. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blamed overloaded routers in an aging air traffic control system. The agency reopened the airspace at eight fifteen in the evening, Eastern time. The flight tracker FlightAware counted more than eleven thousand flights delayed or canceled by nine o'clock. That included more than six thousand cancellations. The ground stop followed communication failures at several air route traffic control centers. Those facilities direct high-altitude traffic between airports. Duffy said the routers got overloaded with data and began feeding inconsistent information. He said the system is operational now. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said preliminary investigations found no link to an attack. He called it a telecommunications outage. Officials are examining incompatibilities between older equipment and newer technology. Passengers already aboard aircraft sat on the tarmac for hours. Airlines planned around the disruption through the weekend. College and professional teams scrambled to rebook charter flights before weekend games. Duffy used the briefing to press Congress for money to replace the control system. He warned that similar failures could recur without it.

Thirty-two people were taken to local hospitals, none with critical injuries. The Coast Guard said all passengers and crew were accounted for. New Orleans Mayor Helena Moreno said the tanker lost control and hit the riverboat. She called the absence of deaths a miracle on the Mississippi. The American Heritage is a paddlewheel vessel built in twenty fifteen. It had been docked for about two hours, scheduled to begin a seven-night cruise up the river. Passenger Rhonda Akers said the tanker struck the boat twice before it rolled over. She said she had to crawl over the railing and other passengers to save herself. 6|Also in politics, the Trump administration announced Friday a six-month delay in enforcing Medicaid work requirements. The rules were scheduled to take effect in January. Under the rules, enacted in last year's budget law, adults without dependent children must work, attend school or volunteer at least eighty hours a month. Otherwise they must claim an exemption to keep coverage. States that opt into the delay still begin compliance checks on January first. But no one loses coverage until July. The pause follows months of warnings from state officials that the rollout was moving too fast. A federal judge blocked part of the rule last month, ruling that states lacked time and resources. Nebraska's early start produced confusion and coverage denials. State figures through August showed more than a thousand people lost coverage or were denied benefits. The Congressional Budget Office estimated the requirements would leave five point three million more people uninsured by twenty thirty-four. Some venture investors expect Google, Amazon, Microsoft and the chipmaker Advanced Micro Devices to weigh backing startups that drive adoption of their own products. 8|Over in technology, SpaceX launched the fifteenth flight of its Starship rocket from Starbase, Texas on Friday evening. Liftoff came at five forty-seven, Eastern time. The orbiter, named Ship forty-two, reached orbit carrying twenty-six next-generation Starlink satellites. But the planned catch of its Super Heavy booster failed. The booster was due back at the launch pad about six and a half minutes after liftoff. Engine trouble forced it into a splashdown in the Gulf of Mexico instead of the tower's mechanical arms.
