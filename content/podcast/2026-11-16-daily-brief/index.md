2|Our first story takes us to Prague, where huge crowds filled Letna park on Sunday. 4|In other news from the Americas, Miguel Angel Felix Gallardo has died at the age of eighty. He was the former police officer who built the Guadalajara cartel and welded Colombia's cocaine trade to Mexico's smuggling corridors. The security ministry of Jalisco said he died Sunday in a hospital in that state, two days after his transfer there from prison. His body shut down in multiple organ failure. His cartel imploded in the nineteen nineties, and its successors produced the Sinaloa cartel. He was granted house arrest in twenty twenty-two, but a faulty ankle monitor kept him behind bars.
