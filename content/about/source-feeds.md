Seven Lights finds its stories by reading the public RSS feeds of the news organizations listed on this page. Nothing else is scanned: no social media, no press releases, no anonymous sites. The list below is generated directly from the live configuration the publishing software uses, so it always shows exactly what the site reads.

Three points a reader should know:

- Appearing on this list is not an endorsement. The feeds are read for facts, and every published summary links back to the original articles so a reader can check the reporting behind each claim.
- A feed is only where a story starts. A story is published here only when at least three separate articles from at least three different media companies report it, as stated on the [About page](/about/). One outlet's story, however well written, does not run.
- The list changes over time. Feeds are added when they carry reliable, accessible reporting, and removed when they stop updating or block automated reading. Removed candidates are tested before they are ever added.
